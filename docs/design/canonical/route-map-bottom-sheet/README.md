## Locked behavior

- One interactive screen: a full-screen map behind a draggable bottom sheet with collapsed, half-open, and expanded snap points.
- The map remains visible in every sheet state. Follow the final dark reference heights: collapsed around 29% of viewport, half-open around 66%, and expanded around 80%. The collapsed state preserves the compact trip summary, colored time-proportional bar, and horizontal mode/fare chips.
- The same segment is selected from its map line, timeline interval, or card. Selection updates the map focus and the matching card together; it never changes the itinerary, schedule, fare, or booking state.
- The map has two route-display states: `OVERVIEW` shows every leg of the selected journey in the canonical mode colors; `SEGMENT_FOCUS` shows only the selected journey leg(s) on the map. Other journey legs are hidden from the map in focus state, but remain in the itinerary and bottom sheet.
- Segment geometry is spatially truthful. Use the provider/router road or transit shape for each leg and preserve its real coordinates and length; do not stretch a short leg, normalize all legs to equal lengths, or derive map length from duration. The 3 km part of a route should occupy approximately three times the route distance of a 1 km part at the same map scale, subject to real road geometry and map projection. The timeline bar remains a separate display and is proportional to elapsed time, including waits.
- Visual line width and touch target are separate. Keep the line on its real geometry and preserve the reference's clear color treatment. Give each selectable line/segment marker a minimum 44×44 logical-point hit target without changing the line's geographic length. Resolve overlapping hit targets by the nearest rendered route geometry; if two candidates remain ambiguous, zoom to the overlap or present a small explicit segment choice instead of silently selecting the wrong leg. Segment cards and timeline intervals remain equivalent selection controls.
- Selecting a scheduled bus, minibus, tram, trolleybus, metro, or train enters `TRANSIT_LINE_FOCUS`: smoothly zoom and center on the selected itinerary portion; hide all other journey legs from the map; keep the selected portion bright in its category color; and progressively reveal the complete matching transit line beyond both the planned boarding and alighting stops in both directions, using the same color at low opacity. This contextual full-line overlay is not an itinerary leg and must not appear in the bottom-sheet itinerary.
- In `TRANSIT_LINE_FOCUS`, show available live vehicles for that exact provider route in both directions as circular markers with the matching transport icon. Keep vehicles that are not approaching the selected boarding stop subdued. Emphasize up to the three most relevant vehicles that are actually traveling toward that stop, ranked by a provider ETA when available or by a validated position/direction estimate. Do not infer direction or ETA from route name alone. Retain the selected vehicle route's line geometry and the planned leg's bright overlay so the user can distinguish the chosen itinerary from the whole line.
- Realtime markers require a fresh, route-correlated provider position. Respect feed timestamps; data older than 90 seconds is stale for the live-emphasis state. Do not animate a stale vehicle as if live, invent missing vehicles, or show a fabricated ETA. If provider route identity, direction, or freshness cannot be established, keep that marker neutral or omit it and explain that live positions are unavailable. Scheduled stop times remain visibly distinct from realtime estimates.
- Selecting an unbooked private carpool offer enters `PRIVATE_RIDE_FOCUS`: hide the other journey legs from the map and focus the offered road path between its actual pickup and drop-off. Keep the corresponding card and its purple accent active. Show the offer's real pickup/drop-off, departure/arrival, price per seat, and confirmed available seats. Do not draw a fictional two-way transit line or unrelated cars for a private offer. Before booking confirmation, never expose the driver's private live GPS.
- A confirmed carpool booking must provide a dedicated `CARPOOL_RENDEZVOUS_FOCUS` before boarding; this is a required part of the route experience, not an optional enhancement. Focus the map on the agreed pickup point and the current distance between the two participants. Both passenger and driver see their own live location, the other participant's fresh shared location, the fixed meeting point, and the two approaches to that point. Show each participant's distance to pickup and a route-based ETA when routing and fresh location data support it. The bottom sheet identifies “Водій до посадки” and “Пасажир до посадки”, shows live/stale/unavailable state and the scheduled meeting time, and retains the whole itinerary in its cards. Keep the planned carpool ride from pickup to drop-off as a separate route leg; it is not the rendezvous approach. When boarding is confirmed, end rendezvous focus and show the planned ride leg toward drop-off.
- Rendezvous sharing is limited to the confirmed booking participants and the product's defined pre-pickup sharing window. Request device geolocation permission, transmit only real coordinates, preserve capture timestamps and accuracy, and stop/expire sharing when the meeting ends or booking is cancelled. If either participant has not shared a fresh position, show their position as unavailable or stale and explain why; never synthesize a marker, route, distance, or ETA. A confirmed booking must still open the rendezvous map state when GPS is denied or unavailable, with the meeting point and an explicit location-permission/status explanation.
- Selecting walking, scooter, bicycle, taxi, carsharing, or transfer focuses the selected leg's actual provider/router geometry and endpoints; other itinerary legs are hidden from the map. Do not add public-line or fleet overlays unless a real matching provider feed supports them.
- Animate focus transitions smoothly and cancel obsolete camera/line-reveal animations when the selection changes. Account for the bottom sheet: center the focused geometry in the unobscured map viewport, not behind the sheet. A direct map tap and a timeline/card tap must produce the same selected segment and focus state.
- Scheduled transit cards have explicit expand/collapse controls. In the expanded state, open all scheduled transit stop lists by default. When the data source provides stop calls, show every stop in order with its published times. If it does not, disclose that the list is unavailable.
- Each segment shows its own price state. Missing fares remain unknown; estimated fares remain marked approximate.
- MARSHGO private rides use a purple accent and booking action. The button opens the existing offer details/booking flow; it does not book immediately.
- Do not add vehicle or driver photos to this route screen, separate map/detail pages, or per-card “На карті” buttons.
- Keep the route colors consistent in the map, timeline, card accents, and markers. Use exact route identity (provider + route ID and direction/trip when supplied) to join itinerary legs, full-line geometry, and vehicles; do not join ambiguous routes by display name alone. Do not draw guessed geometry, stops, live positions, or availability.
- Preserve accessible labels, keyboard-operable sheet snapping, and touch targets.

The final dark screenshots define the current dark layout and color treatment; earlier dark shots remain archived design history. Their illustrative route values are not production data; render only values and geometry returned by real MARSHGO/provider data. The screenshots lock the overview appearance and segment color language; this interaction contract records the owner's later, more specific map-focus behavior and takes precedence over the older generic instruction to dim every non-selected itinerary leg.

## Interaction state contract

| State | Planned journey legs on map | Transit line context | Vehicle markers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OVERVIEW` | All legs, true geometry and mode colors | None unless the user-enabled transport layer independently requests it | No unrelated fleet overlay |
| `TRANSIT_LINE_FOCUS` | Selected planned transit portion only, bright | Full exact route in both directions, muted | Same-route live vehicles both directions, subdued; up to 3 verified vehicles toward boarding emphasized |
| `PRIVATE_RIDE_FOCUS` | Selected unbooked offer road path only, purple | None | No private live locations |
| `CARPOOL_RENDEZVOUS_FOCUS` | Agreed pickup point and actual participant approaches; the post-pickup ride is not the active map focus | None | Both confirmed participants' fresh, authorized positions; distance/ETA to pickup per participant |
| `CARPOOL_RIDE_FOCUS` | Confirmed planned ride path from pickup toward drop-off | None | Only locations authorized for the in-progress booking |
| `OTHER_SEGMENT_FOCUS` | Selected leg only | None unless that leg has a real matching provider line | Only data belonging to the selected provider/leg |

Clearing focus returns to `OVERVIEW`. Hiding itinerary legs on the map never removes them from the route data, timeline, cards, stop lists, or trip summary.
